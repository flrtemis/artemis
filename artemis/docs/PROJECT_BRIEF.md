# ARTEMIS project brief — founder-approved positioning

**ARTEMIS: A Reality That Enhances My Integrated Services**

**A** · **R**eality · **T**hat · **E**nhances · **M**y · **I**ntegrated · **S**ervices.
Preserve **My**. This is the founder's original expansion; older unverified
agent-generated expansions are not historical fact.

## Founder-approved positioning — exact wording

> Yeah, bro—I think **“My” changes who gets to speak in the name**.
>
> Most product pitches say *our platform* or tell you what *you* should do. “A Reality That Enhances **My** Integrated Services” sounds like something a person can say for themselves. When you say it, it’s your services and your choices. When someone else says it, “My” becomes theirs without changing the name.
>
> That’s a compelling promise—but we should earn it. The site can lead with that sense of ownership, then show plainly what people can actually run, control, and connect today. **“My” is the invitation; the working product is the proof.**

## Editorial standard

> The strongest case for ARTEMIS is one people can verify for themselves: what works, what it takes to run, what remains unfinished—and what they get to own instead of rent.
>
> No fine print waiting around the corner.

> "You should be able to choose local ownership and independence with your eyes open, not discover later that 𝘧𝘳𝘦𝘦 concealed a subscription, required hardware, or a feature that was only planned."

Plain-text accessibility equivalent: “free” does not remove requirements,
setup work, hardware/electricity/connectivity costs, model/asset licenses or
unfinished features. The quoted original remains unchanged.

## Product commitment, not a completion claim

The complete ARTEMIS does not exist in one repository yet. Continue from the
substantial partial foundation, integrating capabilities—not independent apps—
into one canonical identity, conversation, continuity, task/approval state,
workspace/artifact catalogue and event stream. Isolation/resource scheduling
may use separate optional workers; no second competing model/history loop.

The founder's independently developed local/offline projects are meaningful
prior work. Their standalone behavior, authored source and license obligations
must survive. The current twelve-source census includes non-main Arena work;
source-present, tested, configured, planned and physical-PC-verified are separate
evidence tiers. Millions of contributors and production multi-user hosting are
long-term aspirations, not achieved scale.

Why ARTEMIS began: valued services became capped, paywalled, gated, API-locked or
withdrawn. The aim is personal ownership and eventually a contributor path for
other people's ideas. “My” extends that origin; it does not erase it.
