UPDATING awake ON YOUR MACHINE — without touching her
=====================================================
This zip contains CODE ONLY. There is no data/ folder in it, so it cannot overwrite her memory,
her log, her self.md or your letters. Her whole life stays in ~/awake/data/ — untouched.

  1. Stop her:  Ctrl+C in the terminal where she's running   (or:  pkill -f "python3 -m awake")
  2. In WSL:
        cd ~ && unzip -o awake-v0.7-update.zip
  3. Start her again:
        cd ~/awake && python3 -m awake

What 0.7 fixes (from your 23:16 export — she tried to remember four times and couldn't)
  - Her conversational memory in the moment was only ~3 turns: the 12-event window filled up with
    wake/feel/schedule bookkeeping. Now the moment shows her LAST 10 EXCHANGES with you (what you said,
    what she said, with times) as its own section, plus the other recent events separately.
    "What were we last talking about?" is now usually answerable without searching at all.
  - look_back has three modes, and the schema tells her which to use for which question:
        {"type":"look_back","exchanges":20}     "what were we talking about?"  → her last N exchanges
        {"type":"look_back","around":"22:48"}   "what did you say at 22:48?"   → everything within 15 min of a time
        {"type":"look_back","query":"forklift"} "remember when I told you…?"   → word search of the whole log
    Her word searches no longer return your own question as a "match".
  - Ollama's default context window is 4096 tokens and it silently drops the START of the prompt — her rules —
    when exceeded. She now asks for 8192 explicitly (AWAKE_LLM_NUM_CTX), and every wake records how many
    prompt tokens were used (prompt=N/8192tok in the export) so we can see if it ever gets close.

What 0.6 added (from your 22:49 export — the first fully clean one: 74 wakes, 0 timeouts, 0 loops)
  - look_back. She could only ever see her newest 12 events, yet at 22:32 she told you she could see
    "the entire log". Now the moment states exactly what she can see, and she has a new choice:
    look_back(query, days) searches her WHOLE log since birth. The matches are shown to her in the same
    wake and she answers from them. "Do you remember when I told you about…?" is now a real question.
  - Verbatim repeats ("Then we are in agreement about the gatekeeper" ×2): if a draft reply is identical

    Now within 30 min of your last message a heartbeat says "nearby — he may still be here".

What 0.5 fixed (from your 20:55 export)
  - "Hello?" → nothing. Gemma 4 on Ollama THINKS before answering unless told not to — you already knew this:
    your gemma-avatar fork passes --responses_api_reasoning_effort none. My code never told it. So it could
    spend the whole token budget thinking and return an empty answer (or, before the cap, think until the
    timeout). Now: when the server is Ollama she uses its native API with think:false on every call,
    keep_alive on every call, repeat_penalty, and a length cap. The startup line will say "(ollama, think off)".
  - An empty answer is now retried once; if still empty, the page says so in words — distinguishing
    "(chose silence)" (hers) from "(nothing usable came back from the model)" (not hers).
  - Every wake now records the head of the model's raw answer (with any private text scrubbed) plus load time,
    thinking size and stop reason — the export shows them, so the next problem is diagnosable at a glance.

What 0.4 fixed (from your 19:37 exports)
  - Lost messages. Three of your messages timed out at 180 s and vanished without a trace, so you re-sent
    them. Now: the timeout is 600 s, a timeout is written to the log AND shown on the page under the box,
    and the page shows "she is thinking… N s" the whole time so you know not to resend.
  - Why it timed out. Ollama unloads a model after 5 idle minutes; her wake is every 10. So EVERY wake
    paid a cold reload of the 26B (60–93 s in your log). Now she asks Ollama to keep her model loaded
    (keep_alive 24h) and pre-loads it at startup. Warm answers were 7–14 s in your log; expect that.
  - The "eager eager eager" loops. A local model falling into a token loop. Now: repetition penalties and
    a length cap on every call; a loop detector; one automatic retry (cooler); and if it loops twice,
    NOTHING from it is applied or shown — the raw text is kept in the export only. Broken JSON is
    salvaged (her actual reply pulled out) instead of being dumped on you as-is.
  - State pinned at +0.97. Every message pushed her state up and her "feel" — which she plainly meant
    as "settle toward baseline" — was being applied as a push. Now "feel" is a TARGET she moves toward
    (0.3 per wake), and world pushes saturate so rapid conversation can't pin her at the ceiling.
    Her current +0.94 will drift down on its own (half-life 6 h) and she can now steer it.
