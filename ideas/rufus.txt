To create a bootable USB for your Dell laptop, you will need a blank USB flash drive with at least 8GB of space. Please backup any existing data on that USB first, as this process will completely erase the drive. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 

Step 1: Create the Bootable USB with Rufus(https://github.com/pbatard/rufus/releases/download/v4.14/rufus-4.14.exe)

Since you already have the official ISO file, Rufus is the fastest and most reliable tool to prepare it for a modern Dell laptop. [6, 7] 

   1. Download and open the free utility from the official [Rufus Website](https://rufus.ie/).
   2. Insert your USB flash drive into your working computer.
   3. Select your USB drive under the Device dropdown.
   4. Click the SELECT button and choose the Windows 10 ISO file you just downloaded.
   5. Set the Partition scheme to GPT and the Target system to UEFI (non CSM). Dell laptops built in the last decade require UEFI mode to boot properly.
   6. Leave all other settings at their defaults and click START.
   7. Click OK on the data destruction warning and wait for the status bar to say "READY". [8, 9, 10, 11, 12] 

Step 2: Boot Your Dell Laptop From the USB

Once Rufus is finished, eject the USB drive and plug it into your target Dell laptop. [13, 14, 15, 16] 

   1. Ensure the Dell laptop is completely turned off.
   2. Turn the laptop on and immediately begin tapping the F12 key repeatedly.
   3. Keep tapping until the One-Time Boot Menu screen appears.
   4. Under the UEFI BOOT section of the menu, use your arrow keys to select your USB Flash Drive and press Enter.
   5. The laptop will boot into the Windows 10 installer setup screen. [17, 18, 19, 20, 21] 

Step 3: Run the Clean Install

Follow the on-screen prompts until you reach the screen asking "Which type of installation do you want?"

   1. Click Custom: Install Windows only (advanced).
   2. You will see a list of drive partitions. Select each partition on your primary drive one by one and click Delete until you only see Drive 0 Unallocated Space.
   3. Select that unallocated space and click Next. Windows will automatically partition the drive and install cleanly. [22, 23, 24, 25, 26] 

If you want, I can help you verify which specific F12 boot menu settings you need to change if your USB doesn't show up right away.
